Jewellery Care

Water & showering

Most pieces are water-resistant and safe for handwashing and light rain. For anything marked fade-resistant only, remove before showering or swimming.

Storage

Keep pieces separate in a soft pouch or lined box to avoid scratching. Avoid direct sunlight for long periods.

Cleaning

A soft, dry cloth after wear is usually enough. For a deeper clean, lukewarm water with a drop of mild soap, then dry thoroughly before storing.
